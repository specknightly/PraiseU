# Entropy Shield WorkRecord v2.0.1

## Career Evidence Focus

v2.0.1 re-centers WorkRecord around the two professional-memory problems it is uniquely positioned to solve:

1. **Promotion evidence** — preserve wins with enough context and evidence to support reviews, raises, promotions, title changes, and role reclassification.
2. **CYA incident documentation** — preserve contemporaneous factual records of uncomfortable workplace events, disputes, failures, escalations, and complaints.

Lumina remains the Entropy Shield general notes/reminders application. WorkRecord deliberately stays narrower.

## Quick Capture

Quick Capture now has two explicit modes:

- **Win**
  - title,
  - what you did / what changed,
  - metric/reference/evidence note,
  - saved as a draft accomplishment tagged for promotion evidence and later review.

- **Incident**
  - title,
  - observed facts,
  - people/system/ticket/email/evidence reference hint,
  - saved as a draft incident tagged for CYA review.

Quick Capture is intentionally a starting point, not the final evidentiary record. The UI tells the user to finish the record in WorkRecord and attach source evidence.

## Main-window language

The two sides of WorkRecord are now framed as:

- **Promotion Evidence**
- **CYA Incident Record**

Primary creation buttons are:

- **Record Win**
- **Document Incident**

The application continues to use the professional Accomplishment and Incident data models underneath those labels.

## Roadmap correction

The v2.1–v3.0 roadmap is now career-evidence specific.

Major future milestones include:

- Review Room.
- Evidence Provenance & Confidence.
- Promotion Case Builder.
- Incident Defense Room.
- Evidence Intake & Corroboration.
- Career Trajectory Observatory.
- Incident Pattern & Response Intelligence.
- Career Evidence Integrity & Retention.
- Career Packet Studio.
- Entropy Shield Career Evidence OS.

Every future feature is expected to pass a simple relevance test:

- Does it make a win easier to prove?
- Does it make an uncomfortable incident easier to reconstruct factually?

If neither is true, the feature probably belongs in Lumina or another Entropy Shield application instead.

## Privacy

No runtime incident/accomplishment data or evidence files are part of this release.

The source-only privacy guard remains unchanged and continues to block runtime databases, evidence directories, backups, and common workplace attachment formats.

## Compatibility

No persisted-data schema changes are required for v2.0.1.

Existing incidents, accomplishments, evidence, Work Graph data, prevention records, operational-burden records, role baselines, and Work Intelligence behavior remain compatible.
