# WorkRecord Product Boundary — Career Evidence, Not General Notes

## Position

Entropy Shield WorkRecord is a **local-first career evidence vault**.

It exists for two moments that are usually poorly served by ordinary note-taking applications:

1. **When your work deserves recognition.**
2. **When an uncomfortable workplace event needs a factual record.**

The product should therefore optimize for **proof**, **chronology**, **provenance**, **recall under pressure**, and **professional exports**.

It should not compete with Lumina for general notes/reminders.

---

## The Win side

A win is more than “I did X.”

Promotion-grade evidence usually needs:

- What was the situation?
- What did you personally do?
- What changed?
- What was the measurable or observable outcome?
- Who/what benefited?
- Was this routine work or higher-level responsibility?
- Is there a ticket, email, screenshot, change record, metric, or stakeholder acknowledgment?
- Did this become sustained responsibility rather than a one-off task?
- Did it prevent future work or absorb unusual operational burden?

WorkRecord should make weak wins visibly incomplete and strong wins easy to export.

### Product test

If a feature cannot help answer **“Why should this person be promoted, recognized, reclassified, or trusted with greater responsibility?”**, it is probably not core to the Win side.

---

## The Incident side

An uncomfortable incident is more than “something bad happened.”

A defensible record usually needs:

- Exact date/time.
- Recorded/discovered date/time.
- Observed facts.
- Exact language where reliably remembered.
- People involved.
- Witnesses/corroboration.
- System/location.
- Ticket/email/change/case references.
- What the user did in response.
- What happened next.
- What remains unresolved.
- Evidence preserved while it still existed.
- Separate interpretation from observation.

WorkRecord should make it easy to document these details **contemporaneously**, before memory and organizational narratives drift.

### Product test

If a feature cannot help answer **“What actually happened, what did I do, and what evidence can I show later?”**, it is probably not core to the Incident side.

---

## CYA without turning toxic

“CYA” in WorkRecord means **create a contemporaneous, factual, professional record**.

It does not mean:

- assume bad faith,
- diagnose coworkers/managers,
- infer motives,
- score personalities,
- manufacture blame,
- write inflammatory narratives.

The strongest defensive record is usually the least dramatic:

- dates,
- facts,
- references,
- evidence,
- actions,
- outcome,
- follow-up.

---

## Entropy Shield philosophy applied to career evidence

The public Entropy Shield framework emphasizes reducing information entropy while preserving evidence, decisions, relationships, provenance, and context.

For WorkRecord that translates to:

- Capture the win once; do not rely on year-end memory.
- Capture the incident once; do not keep mentally rehearsing it.
- Preserve the receipts.
- Link related systems/projects/people.
- Separate fact from interpretation.
- Let local AI reconstruct relevant context later.
- Keep generated conclusions inspectable.
- Keep sensitive data local.

The goal is not more notes.

The goal is a **defensible professional memory**.

---

## Relationship to Lumina

Lumina and WorkRecord should share visual language and selected Entropy Shield architecture conventions, but their product roles should remain distinct.

### Lumina

- General notes.
- Reminders.
- Everyday capture.
- Broad personal knowledge.
- General-purpose memory.

### WorkRecord

- Promotion evidence.
- Accomplishments.
- Incidents.
- CYA records.
- Evidence attachments.
- Career trajectory.
- Review/manager/HR-ready packets.

A note that has no career-evidence value belongs in Lumina.

A win or uncomfortable incident that may matter in a review, promotion, dispute, handoff, or future explanation belongs in WorkRecord.
